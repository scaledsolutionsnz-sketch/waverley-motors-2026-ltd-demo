Privacy Policy

How Waverley Motors 2026 Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 2026.

1. Overview

Waverley Motors 2026 Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we handle it, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us — for example, your name, email address and message when you send feedback through our private feedback form, or your phone number when you call us. We do not collect payment details through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, quote or booking request
  • To follow up on feedback you have submitted
  • To keep in touch about work on your vehicle

We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Feedback form

Our private feedback form is processed by a third-party form service (FormSubmit) to deliver your message to our inbox. Only the details you enter are sent. No reCAPTCHA or tracking is used on the form.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website uses minimal cookies. Any analytics used are for understanding general website traffic and are not used to identify individuals.

6. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and only retain it for as long as needed to help you.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at any time.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, call 03 217 9425 or email office@waverleymotors.co.nz.
